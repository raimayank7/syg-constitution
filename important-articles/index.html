About this list

The Constitution has more than 450 articles in force. This list picks the ones that students, exam candidates and newspaper readers meet most often. Each line is a short summary in our own words, not the text of the article. The link at the end of a row opens the page where the article is explained with its neighbours.

Articles everyone should know

  • Article 14: equality before the law.
  • Article 19: the six freedoms, including speech.
  • Article 21: life and personal liberty.
  • Article 32: the right to go to the Supreme Court to enforce Fundamental Rights.
  • Article 51A: the Fundamental Duties.
  • Article 324: the Election Commission.
  • Article 352, 356 and 360: the three emergencies.
  • Article 368: amendment of the Constitution.
