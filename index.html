Common questions

Is this the official text of the Constitution?

No. These are simple explanations in our own words. The official text is published by the Legislative Department of the Government of India at legislative.gov.in.

Who is this site for?

School and college students, candidates for competitive examinations, and any citizen who wants to understand how India is governed.

How current is it?

The pages reflect amendments up to the 106th Amendment (2023) and were last checked on 11 November 2026.

Can I use it for my civics project?

Yes. You may read, print and quote short parts for study. Please write the project in your own words.
